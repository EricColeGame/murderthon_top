## Understanding Core Roles: Runners vs. Killers

At its core, *Murderthon* pits a team of runners against a terrifying killer in a deadly race against time. Knowing the foundational objectives of each side will dramatically shorten your learning curve.

### The Runner Objectives
Runners must complete specific objectives scattered across the arena while keeping their distance from the killer. Your goal is not to eliminate the killer—it is to outsmart, outlast, and escape before the timer hits zero.

*   **Objective Progression:** Interact with generators, control consoles, or exit gates to unlock escape paths.
*   **Team Support:** Unhook, revive, or heal teammates to maintain team numbers and distract the killer.
*   **Stamina Conservation:** Run only when necessary to preserve stamina for high-intensity chases.

### The Killer Objectives
Killers possess superior speed, brutal abilities, and tracking instincts. However, killers must deal with cooldowns and blind spots that clever runners can exploit.

*   **Patrolling:** Check high-value objectives regularly to intercept survivors early.
*   **Pressure Management:** Force runners off objectives and deplete their defensive resources.
*   **Elimination:** Down runners quickly to reduce their collective escape speed and morale.

| Role | Primary Objective | Secondary Objective | Biggest Weakness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Runner** | Unlock and open exit routes | Rescue and support teammates | Low health pool, limited stamina |
| **Killer** | Eliminate all runners | Protect high-traffic objectives | Vulnerable to looping and tight corners |

## Movement and Stamina Mechanics Breakdown

Movement is the backbone of survival in *Murderthon*. Wasting your stamina early during a chase is the single most common mistake new players make. 

To refine your mechanics on platforms like the [official Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com/), mastering keyboard and mouse precision is crucial. When navigating tight indoor corridors or open fields, your movement inputs determine whether you vault safely or take a lethal hit.

### Managing Your Stamina Pool
Your stamina bar governs sprinting, sliding, and vaulting. When your stamina hits zero, your character suffers a brief fatigue penalty, slowing you to a crawl.

*   **Walk when safe:** Always walk when exploring or moving between objectives to keep your stamina full.
*   **Feather the sprint button:** Break line of sight before you stop sprinting so the killer cannot immediately predict your path.
*   **Slide mechanics:** Sliding down inclines or under low obstacles saves precious stamina while preserving forward momentum.

| Movement Action | Stamina Cost | Cooldown / Recovery | Tactical Use Case |

| **Standard Sprint** | 15% per second | 3.5s delay before recharge | Breaking away in straight corridors |

| **Slide Recovery** | 5% flat cost | 1.0s delay | Evading wide horizontal slashes |
| **Crouch Walking** | 0% (recovers stamina) | Continuous recovery | Hiding behind low cover and dampening footsteps |

## Strategic Objective Priority and Map Control

Surviving requires completing objectives efficiently without clumping together. Splitting the killer’s attention is the best way to secure an exit. In any comprehensive **murderthon beginner guide**, learning objective sequencing is treated as a top-tier skill.

### The Three-Phase Match Flow
Every match follows a distinct tempo. Adapting your playstyle to the current phase will keep your team ahead of the timer.

1.  **Early Game (First 2 Minutes):** Locate the nearest low-risk objective and identify nearby vault spots. Do not commit to center-map objectives yet.
2.  **Mid Game (Minutes 2 to 5):** The killer will begin securing knockdowns. This is the moment to tackle high-risk, high-traffic objectives while the killer carries or chases someone else.
3.  **End Game (Exit Gates Activated):** Open multiple gates simultaneously. Avoid grouping up at a single exit switch.

| Phase | Runner Focus | Killer Focus | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Scout & Start** | Safe edge objectives, gather items | Scout high-spawn zones, build meter | Low |
| **Phase 2: Mid-Match Pressure** | Staggered objectives, safe unhooks | Tunnel high-value runners, secure kills | High |
| **Phase 3: Endgame Escape** | Split exit gates, bait chase | Camp exit paths, capitalize on mistakes | Extreme |

## Looping and Evasion Tactics for Beginners

When the killer spots you, panic is your worst enemy. Running in a straight line across open ground will get you eliminated within seconds. Instead, practice the art of "looping"—using structural geometry to waste the killer's time.

Community reports and player experiences highlight several foundational looping strategies that work on nearly every map layout:

### Hugging the Geometry
Always cut your corners as tightly as possible. Killers typically have wider hitboxes and slightly larger turning radiuses, meaning tight turns force them to lose ground or swing wide and miss.

### Window and Pallet Mind Games
*   **Fast Vaults:** Approach windows straight-on at maximum sprint speed to trigger a fast vault animation. Angled approaches result in slow vaults that leave you vulnerable.
*   **Pre-dropping Obstacles:** If your health is already low, drop pallets or barricades early rather than risking a greedy stun that might get you downed.
*   **The Double-Back:** Break line of sight behind high walls, then immediately stop running or reverse direction to catch an overeager killer off guard.

| Evasion Technique | Difficulty | Best Environment | Common Mistake |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tight Looping** | Beginner | Small sheds, square crates | Running too far from the wall |
| **Fast Vault Bait** | Intermediate | Long window corridors | Vaulting at an angle (triggers slow vault) |
| **Blind Corner Double-Back**| Advanced | Indoor hallways, dense fog areas | Misjudging the killer’s swing range |

Following this **murderthon beginner guide** methodology will buy your teammates critical minutes to finish tasks while you distract the hunter.

## Advanced Survival Tips and Common Traps to Avoid

Even after memorizing the basics, many new players hit a plateau due to subtle bad habits. Keep these practical tips in mind during high-stress matches:

*   **Listen to Audio Cues:** *Murderthon* relies heavily on directional sound. The killer’s heartbeat, heavy breathing, and metallic scraping all signal their exact approach vector.
*   **Do Not Heal Under the Hook:** When rescuing a teammate, immediately relocate to a safer spot before initiating a heal. Killers frequently double-back to hook locations.
*   **Avoid Objective Tunnel Vision:** If your heartbeat indicator spikes while you work on an objective, abandon it immediately. Trying to squeeze out that last 5% usually ends in disaster.
*   **Use the Camera Constantly:** Never look straight ahead while running. Rotate your camera backward to watch the killer's swings and react accordingly.

Implementing these steps from our **murderthon beginner guide** will dramatically improve your survival rate and give your team the edge they need to conquer the leaderboards.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most important skill in this murderthon beginner guide?
The most critical skill is stamina management combined with tight camera control. Knowing when to walk to preserve your sprint meter allows you to outlast the killer during extended loops.

### How do I know if the killer has spotted me?
Visual indicators like an intensifying screen vignette, along with a rising heartbeat audio cue, signal proximity. If you hear a sudden musical sting or aggressive footstep tempo, the killer has initiated a direct chase.

### Should I prioritize rescuing teammates or working on objectives?
Balance is key. If a teammate is hooked for the first time, you usually have around 45 to 60 seconds to safely stage a rescue. Use the first 20 seconds to advance a nearby objective before rotating over for a coordinated unhook.

### Why is the killer moving faster than me even when I sprint?
Killers naturally possess a higher base movement speed than sprinting runners. You cannot outrun a killer in a straight line across open terrain; you must break line of sight, vault obstacles, and use sharp corners to escape.
