## Understanding the Core Game Loop and Mechanics

At its foundation, *Murderthon* is an asymmetrical horror chase experience built on the [Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com). The match design centers around objectives: survivors must locate and repair generators while evading the killer, ultimately unlocking exit gates to escape. Conversely, the killer must disrupt objective progress, down survivors, and eliminate all four players before the exits open.

Understanding the timing and physics of interactions is critical. Knowing how long an action takes allows you to make informed risks during active pursuits.

### Interaction Timers and Strategic Impact

| Objective Action | Base Duration (Seconds) | Tactical Impact | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Solo Generator Repair | ~75–80s | Restores trial progress; emits audio cues | High |
| Duo Generator Repair | ~45–50s | Accelerates objective completion rapidly | Medium |

Early match phases dictate pacing. When survivors scatter across the map to tackle separate objectives, the killer is forced to cross large distances to apply pressure. If the killer clusters players early, momentum quickly swings in the killer's favor.

### Pallet and Vent Chaining

Pallets provide valuable stuns, but dropping them too early depletes safe zones for late-game chases. Instead, learn to "loop" structures by hugging tight corners to preserve distance. When approaching a vent, always observe the killer's distance:

- **Tight Looping:** Run flush against walls. Taking wide turns gives the killer a shorter trajectory to close the distance.
- **Vent Jukes:** Use vents only when breaking line of sight. If the killer sees you enter a vent trajectory, they can anticipate your exit and cut you off.
- **Pallet Respect Baiting:** Pause briefly near a pallet without dropping it. Killers often swing or hesitate, buying you an extra rotation around the tile.

| Chase Element | Best Practice | Common Rookie Mistake |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pallet Boards** | Drop only when killer commits to a swing or to avoid a hit | Dropping immediately upon reaching the pallet |
| **Vent Paths** | Slide through when killer is blocked by exterior geometry | Using vents in direct killer line-of-sight |
| **Corners & Pillars** | Hug tightly to preserve movement speed advantages | Running wide around obstacles |
| **Injured State** | Break line of sight; seek isolated healing spots | Running in straight lines through open territory |

### Generator Prioritization Strategy

To avoid a "three-gen" trap—where the final three generators are clustered closely together, making defense effortless for the killer—survivors must prioritize middle generators early.

1. **Assess Map Geometry:** Identify centrally located generators immediately upon spawning.
2. **Break Clusters:** Never finish three outer generators consecutively while ignoring the middle.
3. **Commit or Rotate:** If the killer approaches a 90% completed generator, bait the chase away while a teammate finishes the repair.

## Dominant Killer Strategies: Pressure, Routing, and Tracking

Playing the killer role requires aggressive time management. A killer cannot afford to chase a single skilled runner for multiple minutes while three generators activate simultaneously. Applying calculated **murderthon strategies** ensures continuous pressure across the entire map.

### Chase Commitment Decision Framework

| Chase Duration | Situation Assessment | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Anytime** | Multiple survivors spotted on a single gen | Switch focus to disrupt stacked repair progress |

### Advanced Killer Movement and Mindgames

Community reports highlight that predictable killers rarely win against organized squads. You must hide red stain directions (where applicable) and utilize unpredictable angle cuts:

- **Moonwalking Corners:** Briefly face away from corners when rounding structures to conceal your approach vector and trigger survivor panic.
- **Pallet Forcing:** Drive survivors toward "dead zones"—areas where teammates have already dropped and broken pallets—leaving runners with zero defensive counterplay.
- **Cut-off Routing:** Never follow directly behind survivor footsteps through looping structures. Take alternative doorways or hallways to head off their predictable running path.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most effective murderthon strategies for beginners?
Beginners should focus on learning map layouts and avoiding panic pallet drops. As a survivor, always look behind you during chases to track the killer's movement rather than staring straight ahead. As a killer, avoid tunneling a single elusive survivor for more than 30 seconds; rotating between objectives applies far more pressure.

### How do vents work in Murderthon chases?
Vents serve as rapid relocation tools that break direct line of sight. However, they feature fixed exit locations. If you enter a vent while the killer is tracking you closely, a skilled opponent will anticipate your exit point and cut you off. Use them primarily when line of sight is obstructed by physical walls.

### Why is generator spacing critical to winning as a survivor?
If survivors repair all edge generators first, the final generators will spawn closely grouped together. This creates an easy defensive perimeter for the killer. Prioritizing central generators breaks this grouping and forces the killer to traverse long distances between points during the endgame.
