## Gameplay Mechanics: Survivors vs. M.A.S.K. Killer

Murderthon separates players into two distinct asymmetric roles: the Good Guys (Survivors) and the M.A.S.K. Killer. The gameplay balance hinges on time management, audio cue tracking, and map awareness.

### Role Objectives & Responsibilities

| Role | Primary Win Condition | Critical Abilities & Tasks | Primary Threat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Survivors (Good Guys)** | Repair all required generators and escape through exit gates | - Hit repair skill checks<br />- Rescue teammates from electric chairs<br />- Vault obstacles & manage stamina | M.A.S.K. Killer patrol routes, noise pings, trap placement |
| **The Killer (M.A.S.K.)** | Down and execute every survivor before exit gates power up | - Track scratch marks and sound cues<br />- Down survivors via basic attacks/powers<br />- Secure players in electric chairs | Coordinated rescue teams, sabotaged chairs, pallet stuns |

For survivors, remaining patient is vital. Sprinting leaves glowing tracks that guide the killer straight to your location. For killers, committing too long to chasing a single evasive survivor usually costs two to three finished generators elsewhere on the map. Knowing when to abandon a chase and apply map-wide pressure is an essential skill often highlighted in competitive guides on the **murderthon discord**.

## Server Rules, Etiquette, and Safety

Participating in any large Roblox community requires adherence to common safety standards and server etiquette. When interacting on the **murderthon discord**, keep these rules in mind to maintain good standing and protect your account:

### Community Standards Checklist

- **Never Share Account Credentials:** Official staff will never ask for your Roblox password, security cookies, or two-factor codes. Ignore unsolicited direct messages offering free Shards or special roles.
- **Use Dedicated Channels:** Post bug reports with video logs in the designated reporting channels, keep memes in off-topic rooms, and organize teams exclusively inside looking-for-group lobbies.
- **Respect Matchmaking Etiquette:** When joining competitive voice lobbies, maintain clear comms. Avoid mic-spamming or rage-quitting when matches do not go your way.
- **Verify Third-Party Links:** Phishing attempts commonly disguise themselves as tournament signups or free item links. Double-check all external domains before clicking.

Following these practices ensures you can build reliable groups, trade gameplay feedback, and enjoy the community without disruptions.
