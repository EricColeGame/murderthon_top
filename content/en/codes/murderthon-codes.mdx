## Gameplay Overview: How Murderthon Works

Murderthon is an intense, asymmetrical survival horror game built on the Roblox platform. The core gameplay loop pits a squad of coordinated survivors against a solitary, hyper-lethal killer.

```
       [Survivors]                          [The Killer]
            │                                    │
  ┌─────────┴─────────┐                ┌─────────┴─────────┐
  ▼                   ▼                ▼                   ▼
Repair Generators   Revive Teammates  Hunt Survivors     Secure Kills
  │                   │                │                   │
  └─────────┬─────────┘                └─────────┬─────────┘
            ▼                                    ▼
      Open Exit Gates                      Electric Chairs
```

### The Survivor Objective
Survivors must explore dynamic, atmospheric maps to locate and repair broken generators. Activating the required number of generators powers up the exit gates, opening a path to safety. However, working on machinery creates audio cues, making stealth and awareness essential. If a teammate is downed, survivors must risk their own lives to rescue them before the killer finishes them off.

### The Killer Objective
The killer's singular goal is total elimination. Armed with tracking capabilities and deadly melee weapons, the killer must patrol generator clusters, track player footprints, and incapacitate survivors. Once downed, victims are carried to an **electric chair** to be sacrificed, permanently eliminating them from the round unless freed in time.

## Survivor vs. Killer: Tactical Breakdown

Understanding your role's strengths and weaknesses will drastically increase your win rate. The table below outlines key gameplay dynamics for both sides:

| Gameplay Element | Survivor Strategy | Killer Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Repair generators and escape | Eliminate all survivors before gates open |
| **Stealth Utility** | High (crouching, hiding behind cover) | Low (heavy footsteps, audible terror cues) |
| **Mobility** | Agility, vaulting, tight cornering | High sprint bursts, lunges |
| **Team Synergy** | Critical (split repairs, baiting chases) | Independent (no teammates required) |
| **Win Condition** | At least 1 survivor exits through the gate | All survivors placed in the electric chair |

### Essential Survivor Tips
1. **Split Generator Progress:** Avoid having all survivors group up on a single generator early in the round. Spreading out forces the killer to traverse larger distances.
2. **Break Line of Sight:** When chased, utilize sharp corners and obstacles rather than running in long, straight corridors.
3. **Time Your Rescues:** Do not blindly rush the electric chair immediately after an ally is captured. Wait for the killer to walk away to inspect nearby objectives before going for the unhook.

### Essential Killer Tips
1. **Patrol Generator Triangles:** Identify three generators located close together and focus your patrol path around them to create an impenetrable defensive zone.
2. **Listen for Audio Clues:** Survivor footsteps, failed skill checks, and mechanical tinkering produce distinct sounds that reveal hidden players.
3. **Do Not Tunnel Mindlessly:** If a skilled survivor leads you on a prolonged chase across the entire map, break off and surprise vulnerable players working on objectives.
