## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Murderthon Pierce recommended for beginners?
While beginners can appreciate his straightforward dash ability, **Murderthon Pierce** requires disciplined stamina management and intimate map awareness to extract full value. Novice players often mistime the active dash, leaving themselves stranded in open ground during crucial chases.

### How does Pierce compare to other stealth-focused survivors?
Pierce trades complete silence and stealth for high-speed evasion and structural interaction. If your playstyle leans toward hiding in lockers and evading detection entirely, stealth survivors are preferable. If you prefer active juking, Pierce is the stronger choice.

### Can Pierce stun the killer using his Kinetic Breaker?
Community reports confirm that Kinetic Breaker does not inflict direct damage or hard stuns on killers by default. It is designed purely as an obstacle-clearing mobility dash, though colliding with falling debris can momentarily disrupt certain killer animations.

### What is the biggest mistake players make with Murderthon Pierce?
The most frequent misstep is using Pierce's dash as a standard travel ability outside of combat. With a 24-second base cooldown, getting caught without Kinetic Breaker available leaves Pierce vulnerable to sudden killer ambushes. Always reserve your burst for active pursuits or emergency escapes.
