## The Murderthon Lens Mori: Execution Mechanics

A central highlight of playing the **murderthon lens** is triggering its distinct Mori. In asymmetric horror titles, a Mori is a special execution animation performed directly on a dying survivor, bypassing normal carrying or hooking procedures.

According to community gameplay showcases, the Mask of Lens Mori emphasizes direct, brutal visual themes consistent with its namesake. The animation locks both the killer and the target into a cinematic sequence:

| Execution Stage | Visual Presentation | Tactical Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Domination** | Killer pins the survivor to ground level | Freezes killer inputs for roughly 3–4 seconds |
| **Stage 2: The Strike** | Focused lethal blow targeted at the upper body | Signals location to remaining survivors via audio cues |
| **Stage 3: Conclusion** | Clean disengagement as survivor is removed | Target is permanently eliminated; no unhook possible |

Executing a survivor with the **murderthon lens** removes them entirely from the match economy. This shifts the trial from a 1v4 into a much more killer-favored 1v3, drastically reducing the team's collective generator repair speed.

## Survivor Counterplay: Surviving Against the Lens

Facing a skilled **murderthon lens** user can feel overwhelming if you sprint aimlessly across open terrain. Because Lens relies on solid tracking to win chases, your best defense is breaking line of sight and delaying hits as long as possible.

### Key Survivor Defensive Tips

| Threat | Counter-Tactic | Why It Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Sightlines** | Hug tall geometry and jungle gyms | Denies the killer easy distance closing |
| **Generator Ambush** | Work on edge generators first | Forces the killer to travel longer patrol distances |
| **Tight Looping** | Pre-drop unsafe pallets | Prevents lunges without risking a hit during vault animations |
| **Tracking Persistence** | Walk instead of running when out of sight | Erases scratch marks, confusing the killer's chase path |

Player experience demonstrates that the most effective survivor teams divide repair responsibilities. If one player distracts the **murderthon lens** for even 45 seconds, the other three players can finish multiple generator stages, shifting pressure entirely onto the killer.
