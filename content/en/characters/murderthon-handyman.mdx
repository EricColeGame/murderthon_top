## Match Phase Strategy: Early, Mid, and Late Game

Winning consistently in *Murderthon* requires adjusting your playstyle as the round progresses.

```
Early Game (Scout & Fortify) -> Mid Game (Objective Blitz) -> Late Game (Gate Breach & Escape)
```

### 1. The Early Phase: Setting Up Safety Networks
Do not immediately sprint to the central map objective. Experienced killers frequently check prime generators first. Instead, locate nearby side rooms and fortify alternative escape routes. Use your class bonuses to set reinforced barricades on key door frames.

### 2. The Mid-Game Phase: Safe Objective Completion
Once the killer commits to chasing an agile runner or decoy teammate, capitalize on your repair bonuses. Work alongside another survivor if possible to trigger the buddy speed multiplier. If the killer breaks off their chase, immediately rotate toward your pre-fortified safe rooms.

### 3. The Endgame Phase: Powering Exits
When the final phase begins, the **murderthon handyman** should immediately head to the primary exit switches. With your native interaction speed buffs, you can open emergency exits in roughly two-thirds of the standard duration. This speed often makes the difference between a complete team wipe and a four-player escape.

## Pro Tips to Dominate as the Handyman

1. **Stagger Your Fortifications**: Never place two reinforced barricades right next to each other. Space them out so the killer must perform multiple lengthy destruction animations across different corridors.
2. **Listen for Terror Radii**: Because the **murderthon handyman** operates stationary minigames frequently, train your ears to detect faint audio shifts. Step away from objectives *before* the killer gains visual contact.
3. **Conserve Durability**: Avoid using high-tier tools on minor map barricades. Save your best gear for high-priority generators and the final escape switch.
4. **Coordinate Distractions**: Communicate with your team's runners. A runner leading the killer to the opposite side of the map gives you free rein to complete objectives unhindered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the murderthon handyman unique compared to other survivor classes?
The Handyman is built specifically around objective velocity and environmental manipulation.
