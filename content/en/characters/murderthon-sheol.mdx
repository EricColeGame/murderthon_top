## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I unlock Sheol in Murderthon?
Players can obtain Sheol through the character roster menu using accumulated in-game currency earned from completed matches or through limited-time event unlocks associated with the M.A.S.K. update series.

### What is the most reliable counter to Murderthon Sheol's dash attack?
The most reliable counter to the Shadow Jaunt dash is making a sharp 90-degree turn around solid geometry right as the forward surge begins. The dash locks movement onto a fixed trajectory, making lateral tracking difficult in tight spaces.

### Can survivors stun Sheol during the recovery phase?
Yes. Environmental pallets and stun items remain fully effective during the recovery window, giving your squad extended opportunities to break pursuit and revive fallen teammates.
