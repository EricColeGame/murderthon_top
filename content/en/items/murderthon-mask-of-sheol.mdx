2. **Locate the Three Runes:** Scatter across the map perimeter to find the Obsidian, Cinnabar, and Bone runes hidden inside destructible lockers and storage bins.

4. **Survive the Trial of Silence:** You must survive for 60 seconds inside the hazard zone without sprinting. The **murderthon mask of sheol** spawns on the central altar upon completion.
5. **Extract Successfully:** Picking up the mask is not enough; you must escape through the primary vault door to permanently bind the cosmetic and functional item to your account inventory.

## Counter-Play: Playing Against the Mask of Sheol

If you are playing as the Killer, going up against a survivor running the **murderthon mask of sheol** can be exceptionally frustrating. Their footstep sounds are nearly silent, and conventional aura tracking is significantly dampened.

### Killer Tactics and Strategies

- **Rely on Visual Distortion over Audio:** Because acoustic cues are muffled, watch for subtle environmental changes such as displaced dust particles, swinging doors, and disturbed foliage.
- **Box in Confined Spaces:** The mask reduces max stamina. Force the wearer into long straightaways where they cannot take advantage of rapid vaulting or corner breaking. Once their sprint bar depletes, they are easy targets.
- **Equip Wide-Cone Detection Perks:** Perks that detect broad heartbeats rather than pinpoint coordinate aura tracking bypass the mask's passive 1.5-second proximity shield.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does the Murderthon Mask of Sheol transfer between seasons?
Yes. Once unlocked and bound to your account profile after a successful extraction, the item remains in your inventory across seasonal wipes, retaining its core functional perks and cosmetic skin.

### Can beginners use the Murderthon Mask of Sheol effectively?
While accessible to all players who survive the unlock trial, beginners may struggle with the 10% stamina reduction. It is recommended to pair the mask with stamina-boosting perks until you master sprint conservation.

### How does the Murderthon Mask of Sheol interact with killer terror radiuses?
The mask delays proximity detection by roughly 1.5 seconds. This gives survivors an extra moment to react, crouch behind cover, or break direct sightlines before the killer receives standard visual indicators.

### Is the Mask of Sheol better than the Mask of the Void?
The Mask of the Void provides instantaneous short-range teleportation but suffers from a massive 90-second cooldown. In contrast, the **murderthon mask of sheol** provides constant, passive stealth benefits throughout the match, making it superior for consistent team play and objective control.
