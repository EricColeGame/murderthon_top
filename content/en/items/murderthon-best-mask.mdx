## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the single best mask in Murderthon right now?
Across competitive lobbies, the **Phantom Visage** is widely recognized as the **murderthon best mask** due to its combination of silent sprinting and an 18% bonus to stamina regeneration. It offers the most reliable survivability when evading fast killers.

### Can masks be upgraded to improve their base stats?
Yes. Masks can be upgraded from Rank 1 through Rank 10 using scrap materials and currency earned from completing matches. Upgrades improve stamina economy, lower acoustic footprint, and unlock powerful mastery perks.

### Does the killer’s detection counter my mask perks?
Some high-tier killer loadouts feature sensory abilities that mitigate audio dampening. However, masks like the Phantom Visage and Stalker’s Shroud retain high utility because their stamina recovery and directional awareness perks function independently of the killer's sensory build.

### Are cosmetic masks worth buying?
Purely cosmetic masks offer minimal stat enhancements (often limited to minor panic meter buffers). For competitive play, prioritize saving currency for functional masks like the Stalker's Shroud or Neon Operative before investing in vanity cosmetics.
