## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the Murderthon Mask of Fate prevent all audio detection?
No. The **murderthon mask of fate** significantly dampens your footstep audio radius and crouch-walk noise, but it does not completely eliminate environmental audio cues such as door openings, glass breaks, or item crafting sounds.

### Can the Second Chance Barrier activate multiple times in a single round?
Yes, provided the match lasts long enough. The shield has an internal cooldown of 180 seconds. If you survive three minutes after the initial shield breaks, the defensive barrier will recharge for subsequent engagements.

### Is the Mask of Fate tradeable with other players?
Currently, player experience suggests that relic-grade gear earned through seasonal trials or workshop crafting is bound to your individual profile to prevent account boosting and exploit trading.

### How does the mask interact with speed-boosting consumables?
The stamina burn reduction stacks multiplicatively with energy drinks and adrenaline consumables. Pairing the mask with high-tier sprint tonics allows for continuous sprinting across nearly the entire length of larger maps without fatigue penalties.
