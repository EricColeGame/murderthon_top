## Advanced Combat Tactics for Survivors and Hunters

Making the most of the **murderthon mask of ripe** demands disciplined gameplay. Mismanaging your stamina or engaging opponents too early limits its high-tier scaling.

### Looping and Stamina Management
- **Conserve in the Early Game**: Avoid unnecessary sprinting during the first 60 seconds of a match. Let the timer progress to begin building initial scaling.
- **Micro-Sprinting**: Tap the sprint key around sharp corners. The mask's reduced stamina depletion allows you to maintain peak momentum longer than chasers.
- **Audio Deception**: Because your footstep audio is lowered, abruptly stopping behind high cover often causes pursuers to overshoot your position, assuming you ran further ahead.

### Killer Countermeasures
If you are playing as the hunter against an opponent wearing the **murderthon mask of ripe**, keep these community-tested counter-tactics in mind:
- **Force Early Fights**: Pressure mask users within the first minute before their late-game speed bonuses kick in.
- **Trap Tight Choke Points**: Do not engage in lengthy loop chases around standard obstacles; cut them off at critical doors and corridors.
- **Track Shadows and Visual Cues**: Rely on flashlights and visual dust kicked up by sprinting feet rather than audio cues alone.
