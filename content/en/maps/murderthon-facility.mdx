## Sector Breakdown: Anatomy of the Murderthon Facility

The complex features a subterranean research design split into three distinct security tiers: Sector A (Administration & Security), Sector B (Laboratory & Containment), and Sector C (Ventilation & Sub-levels). Each area demands a unique tactical approach due to varying sightlines and acoustics.

| Sector Name | Primary Hazard | Sightline Distance | Key Strategic Value | Best Escape Vector |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sector A (Admin)** | Blind corners, security doors | Short (5–12m) | Terminal hacks, keycard spawns | Service stairwell to B |
| **Sector B (Labs)** | Open central pit, toxic vats | Medium (15–30m) | Power generators, health crates | Catwalk drop-down |
| **Sector C (Sub-level)** | Ambient machine noise, low light | Very Short (3–8m) | Emergency override switch | Maintenance chute to A |

### Sector A: Administration and Security

Sector A houses the primary control office, reception desks, and executive lockers. While this area offers numerous waist-high obstacles for breaking line of sight, the narrow hallways make flanking difficult. Players should avoid lingering in the main lobby, as footsteps reverberate heavily off the linoleum flooring.

### Sector B: Laboratories and Main Containment

Sector B is the visual centerpiece of the **murderthon facility**. It features a two-story layout surrounding a recessed chamber. 

* **Upper Catwalks:** Excellent for scanning enemy movement, but narrow railings leave runners vulnerable to ranged abilities or cut-offs.
* **Lower Decontamination Chambers:** Provides temporary safety behind timed pneumatic doors, though getting trapped inside guarantees elimination if the killer forces entry.

### Sector C: The Sub-Level Maintenance Shafts

Sub-level routes contain minimal lighting and high industrial noise. Community reports indicate that acoustic masking is highest here; heavy machinery muffles player footsteps, allowing both survivors and killers to execute surprise plays.

## Killer Tactics: Controlling the Murderthon Facility

Dominating the **murderthon facility** as a killer requires territorial control and corridor denial. Because the facility lacks expansive sightlines, audio awareness and intercept routing matter far more than raw pursuit speed.

| Killer Archetype | Optimal Facility Sector | Primary Strategy | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Patrol / Tracker** | Sector B (Catwalks) | Oversee lower objectives from elevation | Overcommitting to ground chases |
| **Ambush / Stealth** | Sector C (Sub-levels)| Exploit machinery hum to hide terror radius | Getting looped around central pillars |
| **Trap / Area Denial** | Sector A (Corridors) | Bottleneck staircases and exit gates | Spreading traps too thin |

### Zone Locking Strategy

1. **Herd Survivors Inward:** Force players out of Sector A offices toward the central containment pit in Sector B where avenues of escape are predictable.
2. **Break Pallets Early:** Break interior partition doors and barriers early in the round to strip survivors of safe vault options during end-game pursuits.
3. **Control Verticality:** Never run down staircases behind a target if a drop-down ledge provides an immediate intercept point on the floor below.

Always maintain visual contact with your pursuer via third-person camera angles when turning around server banks. Vault only when the killer commits to an attack animation to conserve window durability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to escape the murderthon facility?
The fastest escape requires powering all three electrical substations to activate the Sector A blast doors. If the main doors are blocked by the killer, player experience suggests rotating through the Sector C drainage grate as an alternative exit when emergency power kicks in.

### Where do keycards consistently spawn in the murderthon facility?
Keycards generate in three primary spots: the administrative desk in Sector A, the technician lockers near Sector B's cooling tanks, and occasionally inside the toolboxes scattered across Sector C maintenance shafts.

### How do you survive the lower level of the murderthon facility?
Surviving Sector C requires minimizing sprint usage to avoid triggering echo mechanics. Move along the perimeter walls, utilize machinery hums to disguise your movements, and always keep an eye on the ladder wells leading back to Sector A.
