## Overview of the Murderthon West Park Environment

West Park stands apart from indoor environments due to its blend of open fields, dilapidated park structures, and dense foliage. While indoor zones offer tight corridors and obvious choke points, the outdoor terrain here creates unpredictable engagement angles. The killer can flank from wide perimeters, while survivors must juggle limited stamina with strategic vaulting points.

According to player experience across competitive lobbies, West Park heavily rewards map awareness over pure sprint speed. Knowing exactly where props spawn and tracking generator or task locations will keep your squad one step ahead of the hunter.

## Detailed Sector Callouts and High-Value Zones

To survive high-tier matches on the **murderthon west park** arena, clear communication is non-negotiable. Standardizing your team's callouts prevents panicked callouts and keeps your squad aligned under pressure. 

Below is an overview of the four primary operational quadrants within the map:

| Sector Name | Primary Landmarks | Hazard Level | Best Role / Class |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **North Gazebo** | Wooden Pavilion, Stone Benches | Medium | Medic (Safe triage location) |
| **Central Playground** | Jungle Gym, Slide, Sandpit Vaults | High | Evader / Kiter (Infinite potential) |
| **Maintenance Sheds** | Tool Benches, Generator Spawns | High | Handyman (Fast prop crafting) |
| **South Woodlands** | Dense Trees, Broken Perimeter Wall | Low-Medium | Stealth / Objective Runners |

### 1. Central Playground
The Central Playground acts as the mechanical heart of West Park. The jungle gym features high-vault railings that force the killer into prolonged pathing animations. However, community reports highlight that overstaying your welcome here can backfire if the killer cuts off the perimeter gate. Use the sandpit pallets sparingly to preserve resources for late-round chases.

### 2. Maintenance Sheds
The Maintenance Sheds contain vital task terminals and prop spawn pools. Handyman players should prioritize reaching this zone early in the match to assemble defensive barriers and barricades. Keep in mind that line of sight inside the sheds is extremely tight, making audio cues vital.

### 3. North Gazebo and Woodlands
The outer ring consists of the North Gazebo and the South Woodlands. These sectors offer high grass and natural cover, making them ideal transit lanes for Medics attempting to reach downed teammates unnoticed.

## Tactical Looping Routes and Survival Strategies

Looping in this map requires deliberate pathing. Unlike maps with continuous interior walls, the **murderthon west park** map punishes survivors who sprint in straight lines across the central lawn.

### High-Priority Looping Routes

| Route Name | Starting Point | Transition Zone | End / Reset Spot |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Playground Carousel** | Sandpit Vault | Slide Ramp | Gazebo Stone Wall |
| **Shed Shrewdness** | Workshop Door | Barricade Window | Woodland Perimeter |
| **Pond Flank** | Broken Footbridge | Overgrown Willow | North Extraction Gate |

### Survivor Survival Protocol
1. **Never Burn Central Pallets Early:** The pallets surrounding the jungle gym are the most valuable assets in late-game scenarios. Break line of sight using the perimeter trees instead.
2. **Coordinate Objective Repair:** Avoid completing all maintenance tasks on one side of the map. Creating a wide split forces the killer to traverse maximum distances, weakening their patrol efficiency.
3. **Exploit Foliage for Stealth Resets:** The South Woodlands break killer auto-tracking easily. Break line of sight, crouch into the high brush, and change direction immediately.

## Killer Strategy: Controlling West Park

Securing victories as a hunter in **murderthon west park** hinges on zone denial. Because survivors enjoy strong looping sequences in the middle of the arena, smart killers herd prey toward the dead zones along the outer fence.

### Patrol and Control Strategy

| Game Phase | Primary Killer Objective | Tactical Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Game** | Force Pallet Drops | Flush survivors out of the Playground early |
| **Mid Game** | Shed Lockdown | Interrupt Handyman fortifications before traps are set |
| **Late Game** | Gate Pinning | Position between the two operational exit gates |

* **Cut the Field in Half:** Avoid chasing survivors who head straight for the North Gazebo loop unless they are already injured. Instead, rotate through the center to protect clusters of unfinished objectives.
* **Listen for Audio Clues:** The gravel paths in West Park make distinct footsteps compared to the grassy woodland zones. Use spatial audio to track running survivors without needing direct visual confirmation.
* **Trap Choke Points:** Force survivors to vault into blind spots near the maintenance structures.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes the murderthon west park map unique compared to other maps?
The **murderthon west park** environment is defined by its hybrid layout, blending wide outdoor sightlines with dense, self-contained looping structures like the Playground and Maintenance Sheds. This requires survivors to alternate between stealth in the foliage and high-skill mechanical looping around props.

### Which class is best for playing on West Park?
The Handyman and Medic perform exceptionally well. The Handyman can fortify vulnerable objective zones near the sheds, while the Medic can leverage the perimeter brush to pull off discreet revives and keep the squad alive during prolonged chases.

### How should survivors handle the exit gate phase in West Park?
Survivors should avoid opening both gates at the exact same moment if the killer is patrolling the central lane. Designate one runner to distract the killer near the Playground while two survivors complete the gate interaction from the woodland perimeter.
