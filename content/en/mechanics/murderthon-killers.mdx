## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the easiest killer for beginners to play in Murderthon?
*The Butcher* is widely regarded by community reports as the most beginner-friendly choice among all **murderthon killers**. His passive ability allows him to power through pallets without precise execution, and his wide swing radius forgives minor targeting mistakes during intense chases.

### How do you avoid getting spotted by aura-reading killer abilities?
Most aura-reading abilities rely on distance and movement states. Survivors can often mask their presence by entering lockers, crouching behind heavy terrain, or moving between vertical floors, which can confuse directional height indicators.

### Can survivors stun all murderthon killers using pallets?
Yes, every slasher in the game is susceptible to physical pallet stuns. However, certain heavy-class killers recover faster from stuns, while stealth killers may ignore the recovery penalty if their unique passive perk tree has been upgraded.

### How often do killer abilities receive balance adjustments?
Balance adjustments typically roll out alongside major seasonal patches and events. Developers regularly tweak cooldown lengths, sprint decay rates, and hitbox alignments based on feedback from the competitive player base.
