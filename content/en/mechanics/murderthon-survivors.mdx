## Core Roles: Classes for Murderthon Survivors

The foundation of any successful match starts in the lobby with your class selection. Each role brings distinct utility that addresses essential team requirements, such as healing wounded allies, repairing objectives, or stalling the killer.

According to community reports and wiki documentation, survivors split primarily between operational utility and medical preservation. Relying purely on solo maneuvering will leave you vulnerable; balancing your squad composition ensures you have answers to unexpected ambushes.

| Class Name | Primary Role | Key Focus | Best Paired With |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Handyman** | Objective & Engineering | Fast repairs, prop manipulation, defensive fortifying | Medic |
| **Medic** | Sustenance & Recovery | Health stabilization, revives, status cleanse | Handyman / Escort |
| **Scout / Runner** *(Community Pick)* | Distraction & Intel | Looping, killer tracking, stamina management | Medic |

### The Handyman Class

The Handyman is the operational backbone for **murderthon survivors**. Without active objective progression, matches drag on, giving the killer more opportunities to corner isolated teammates. 

- **Objective Speed:** Handymen dramatically cut down repair timers on critical match devices.
- **Prop Manipulation:** Better interaction speeds when barricading entryways or deploying portable cover.
- **Resource Conservation:** Higher chance to trigger skill checks without consuming tool charges.

### The Medic Class

When an encounter goes wrong, the Medic transforms catastrophic hits into recoverable skirmishes. Because health does not freely regenerate, having a dedicated healer is non-negotiable in competitive lobbies.

- **Field Dressing:** Rapid healing speed when interacting with injured players.
- **Clutch Revivals:** Shorter vulnerable windows while bringing downed teammates back onto their feet.
- **Status Reduction:** Certain medic builds can mitigate bleeding or crippling ailments faster than standard baseline rates.

## M.A.S.K System and Mask Variants

Beyond innate character classes, **murderthon survivors** can equip specialized headwear known as Masks. These artifacts provide dramatic passive bonuses, situational modifiers, and risk-reward tradeoffs. 

Finding the right synergy between your class talents and your chosen mask can transform an average player into an elusive survival specialist.

| Mask Name | Primary Perk / Effect | Tactical Advantage | Drawback / Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mask of Ripe** | Vitality & Regeneration boost | Increases baseline resilience and recovery speed | Slightly lower sprint burst |
| **Mask of Fate** | RNG / Clutch Rerolls | Grants random tactical buffs during low-health states | Unpredictable procs |
| **Mask of Jammed** | Tech & Disruption | Interferes with killer tracking / jams audio cues | Visual obstruction or reduced perception |

Players looking to inspect the game engine framework and community assets can reference the official [Roblox platform documentation](https://create.roblox.com/) to learn how custom physics and lighting engines drive tension in asymmetric horror titles.

### Mask Selection Tips for Teams

1. **Avoid Redundant Masks:** Do not run an entire team of fragile utility masks. Balance defensive items like the *Mask of Ripe* with disruptive options.
2. **Coordinate with Talents:** Equip masks that elevate your specific class talents rather than trying to balance out every weakness.
3. **Adapt to Map Hazards:** In darker maps with tight corridors, masks that grant tracking or audio disruption yield much higher survivability.

## Talent Trees: Class vs. M.A.S.K Talents

Talent progression is divided into two distinct tracks: **Class Talents** and **M.A.S.K Talents**. By blending perks from both branches, **murderthon survivors** can build highly customized loadouts tailored to aggressive looping, stealthy objective completion, or selfless support.

| Talent Tier | Class Talent Focus | M.A.S.K Talent Focus | Recommended Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Novice)** | Basic interaction speed (+10%) | Cooldown reduction on mask passives | Class Talent |
| **Tier 2 (Adept)** | Stamina consumption drop (-15%) | Mask trigger threshold expanded | M.A.S.K Talent |
| **Tier 3 (Expert)** | Team aura reveal during crisis | Unique mask effect amplifications | Class Talent |
| **Tier 4 (Master)** | Instant-interaction emergency save | Fatal blow resistance / Last stand | Situational Choice |

### Optimizing Your Talent Loadout

When allocating points into Class Talents, prioritize stamina efficiency first. Player experience consistently shows that running out of breath mid-chase accounts for the vast majority of early-round eliminations. 

For M.A.S.K Talents, focus on nodes that decrease activation delays. Since most mask passives trigger during high-threat scenarios, shaving seconds off their cooldowns ensures they are ready during sequential chases.

## Surviving the Hunt: Mechanics, Props, and Status Effects

Survival is not just about running; it is about reading the room. Environmental props and active status effects govern the tempo of every engagement in the arena.

### Environmental Props

The environment is littered with interactive elements designed to slow the killer's momentum. Knowing how and when to deploy these props separates veteran **murderthon survivors** from novice casualties.

| Prop Type | Primary Function | Duration / Health | Recommended Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heavy Barricades** | Blocks doorway access entirely | Destructible by killer (3-4 hits) | Buy time for objective repairs |
| **Loose Debris / Pallets** | Stuns killer if dropped on impact | Single-use drop | Breaking line-of-sight mid-chase |
| **Lockers / Hiding Spots** | Breaks direct vision cone | Infinite (risk of grab) | Evading passing search sweeps |

### Managing Status Effects

Throughout a match, both killers and environmental hazards can inflict debilitating status effects. Understanding their icons and counters ensures you do not waste resources when afflicted.

| Status Effect | Visual / Gameplay Impact | Primary Counter | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jammed / Disrupted** | UI HUD flicker, impaired audio cues | Move away from the source; wait out timer | Moderate |
| **Exhausted** | Sprint disabled, heavy breathing | Walk or crouch; refrain from vaulting | High |
| **Hemorrhage / Bleeding** | Leaves visible blood trails, periodic damage | Medic treatment, basic first-aid bandage | Critical |
| **Terrified (Aura)** | Killer location obscured, erratic heartbeat | Regroup with teammates, break proximity | Moderate |

## Actionable Strategy: A Step-by-Step Survival Checklist

To consistently escape, adopt a disciplined protocol during each phase of the match.

1. **Early Game (0:00 - 2:00):**
   - Disperse silently across separate objective zones.
   - Handymen should immediately locate primary generators or puzzle consoles.
   - Medics must secure nearby medical kits and note the location of choke points.
2. **Mid Game (2:00 - 6:00):**
   - Communicate killer sightings immediately.
   - Rotate away from partially completed objectives if the killer patrols nearby.
   - Use props conservatively; never waste a stun drop if you still have stamina to turn a corner.
3. **Late Game / Endgame Collapse:**
   - Coordinate exit switches simultaneously to force the killer to split attention.
   - Have a healthy survivor body-block or distract the killer if a teammate is carrying debilitating status effects.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best classes for murderthon survivors?
The most reliable combination is running a Handyman alongside a Medic. The Handyman accelerates objective completion speeds to minimize match length, while the Medic provides crucial recovery options to counteract bleeding and unexpected damage.

### How do M.A.S.K items work in Murderthon?
M.A.S.K items are equippable artifacts—such as the *Mask of Ripe* or *Mask of Fate*—that grant passive survival benefits and unique tactical triggers. They can be further augmented through the M.A.S.K Talent tree to provide emergency bonuses during chases.

### What should murderthon survivors do when afflicted with the Jammed status effect?
When hit with the Jammed effect, your HUD and directional audio become distorted. Break line of sight immediately, avoid vaulting unpredictably, and rely on physical landmarks to navigate until the disruption timer expires.
