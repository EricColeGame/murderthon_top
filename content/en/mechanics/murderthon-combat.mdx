## M.A.S.K Killer Offensive Mechanics

Playing as a M.A.S.K requires patience and ruthless precision. Because survivors can utilize third-person camera angles to peek around corners, an effective killer must employ deceptive pathing, attack buffering, and spatial zoning.

### The Attack Hierarchy

Executing attacks in *Murderthon* involves balancing quick swipes against extended lunge attacks. Lunges give extended range at the cost of longer recovery frames if you misjudge the survivor's movement.

### Mind Games and Breaking Line of Sight

The most lethal killer technique in high-level **Murderthon combat** is the red stain hide (also known as the "moonwalk" or "double-back"). The killer projects a subtle visual threat indicator ahead of their model. Smart killers hide this warning by facing walls while walking backward around high-walled corners, tricking survivors into running directly into their weapon swing.

## Survivor Defensive Combat: Looping, Pallets, and Stuns

Survivors do not carry lethal weaponry against the M.A.S.K, but they possess tools that function as direct combat deterrents. Community reports highlight that mastering the interaction between window vaults and structural pallets is essential to surviving long trials.

### The Anatomy of a Loop

Looping is the art of leading a killer around an environmental obstacle (such as a truck, stacked crates, or building ruins) to waste as much of their time as possible while teammates repair Westcord Inc. generators.

| Defensive Resource | Stun Duration | Reset / Exhaustion | Ideal Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wooden Pallet Drop** | 2.2 Seconds | Consumed on use | Drop just as the killer steps into the frame |
| **Fast Window Vault** | 0.0 Seconds | Requires 2.5s sprint run-up | Use before the killer enters lunge distance |
| **Medium Vault** | 0.0 Seconds | Angled approach penalty | Risky; susceptible to vault hits |
| **Flashlight Blind** | 1.8 Seconds | Battery depletion (~12s total) | Blind killer during pallet break animations |
| **Locker Decoy** | 0.0 Seconds | 30s Cooldown | Create audio cue to divert killer attention |

### Pallet Respect vs. Greed

In high-pressure situations, killers often stop short ("respect") when approaching an active pallet, anticipating that the survivor will pull it down immediately. 

- **Greedy Looping:** If the killer stops short, run around the structure one more time without dropping the pallet. This wastes an extra 3 to 4 seconds of the killer's time.
- **Pallet Stun:** If the killer lunges aggressively through the opening, pull the pallet down directly onto their head. This triggers a 2.2-second stun, completely resetting the chase and clearing any built-up bloodlust momentum.

## Essential Tactical Strategies for Combat Scenarios

To consistently win trials, both sides must adapt their execution based on remaining objectives, available map resources, and player health states.

### Survivor Strategy: The 360 Spin Counter

When caught in a wide-open area with no pallets or windows nearby, an injured survivor's primary mechanical defense during **Murderthon combat** is the rotational spin. 

1. Watch the killer approach using your free-look camera.
2. The split second the killer begins their swing audio or wind-up animation, sprint sharply toward the killer's non-dominant side.
3. Rapidly rotate your movement keys while dragging your camera in the opposite direction.
4. If performed correctly, the killer's forward momentum will carry their blade harmlessly over your shoulder, locking them into a punishing whiff animation.

### Killer Strategy: Objective-Based Combat Control

Chasing a skilled survivor endlessly across the map is the easiest way to lose a match. Elite killers apply strict pressure triage:

```
[Is Chase Under 20s?]

      ├── YES ──► [Commit & Secure Hit]

      └── NO  ──► [Check Nearby Generators]
                        │
                        ├── Survivors Present? ──► [Switch Target]
                        └── All Quiet?          ──► [Kick & Patrol]
```

- **Drop Low-Value Chases:** If a survivor reaches a formidable loop (such as a double-window warehouse), abandon the chase immediately.
- **Patrol the Triangle:** Identify three generators located close to each other. Defend this cluster aggressively and force encounters within this tight perimeter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if the killer catches me in the open during Murderthon combat?
If you are caught away from pallets or windows, avoid running in a straight line. Use irregular serpentine zig-zags to throw off the killer's aim. As they close into lunge range, execute a tight 360-degree spin into their flank to capitalize on their attack lock-out frames.

### How does attack recovery affect Murderthon combat engagements?
When a killer misses an attack, their movement speed drops to approximately 5 studs per second for up to 1.8 seconds. This window gives survivors enough time to reach a nearby vault or pallet. Conversely, landing a hit allows the survivor to gain a 2-second speed burst, resetting the distance between both players.

### Can survivors fight back and eliminate the killer?
No. In *Murderthon*, survivors cannot kill the M.A.S.K. The combat dynamic is entirely defensive and evasive. Survivors fight back using environmental hazards, pallet stuns, flashlight blinds, and coordinated body-blocks to protect vulnerable teammates and escape the trial.
